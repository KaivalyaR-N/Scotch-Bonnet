Scotch Bonnet Specials

At Scotch Bonnet , we are passionate about bringing the bold and vibrant flavors of the Scotch Bonnet chili to your everyday snacks. Known for its fiery heat and fruity, tangy flavor, the Scotch Bonnet chili is the heart of all our products, from crunchy wafers to tangy pickles. We specialize in infusing this unique pepper into a variety of snack foods, creating a taste experience that’s as exciting as it is bold.

✠ Our Current Products ✠

♦Scotch Bonnet Chili Wafers:: Our Scotch Bonnet chili wafers take the classic crispy snack to a whole new level. Infused with the fiery heat and fruity flavor of the Scotch Bonnet, these wafers deliver a bold and exhilarating flavor with every bite. Whether you're enjoying them on their own or with your favorite dip, they're perfect for spice lovers looking for something exciting.

♦Scotch Bonnet Chili Nachos:: Our nachos are loaded with the distinct heat of Scotch Bonnet chili, giving your snack time an extra kick! The crunchy tortilla chips, combined with the bold chili flavor, make these nachos a spicy and satisfying treat. Pair them with your favorite toppings or enjoy them straight from the bag—either way, they’re guaranteed to turn up the heat!

♦Scotch Bonnet Chili Fryums:: Our fryums are a crispy, puffed snack that packs a punch! The fiery Scotch Bonnet chili infuses each fryum with intense heat and flavor. Light, crunchy, and full of spice, these fryums are the perfect snack for those who love the thrill of a spicy treat that’s both addictive and bold.

♦Scotch Bonnet Chili Pickles:: Take your pickle experience to the next level with our Scotch Bonnet chili pickles. The heat of the chili complements the tanginess of the pickled vegetables, creating a zesty, spicy snack that pairs well with meals or stands alone as a fiery treat. Ideal for adding some heat to sandwiches, rice dishes, or enjoying straight from the jar!

Expanding Our Flavor Frontier But we’re not stopping here! At Scotch Bonnet, we’re always experimenting and working on new and exciting ways to incorporate the bold flavor of Scotch Bonnet chili into snacks. Our team is actively developing even more innovative products, including new variations of wafers, spicy seasonings, and more fiery treats, all designed to bring the unique heat of Scotch Bonnet chili to a wider range of snack lovers. We are committed to crafting more products that challenge your taste buds and satisfy your craving for something spicy, delicious, and truly one-of-a-kind.
